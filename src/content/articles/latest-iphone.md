## The quick answer

Apple announces new iPhones every September. Because model names and availability change with each release, the single reliable source is **Apple's own iPhone page** for your country.

## How to tell which model is newest

- **Check the release date**, not the marketing name. Apple sometimes keeps older models on sale at lower prices.
- **Compare chip generations.** The latest model ships with Apple's newest A-series chip.
- **Regional differences matter.** Some models launch in certain markets first, and camera or SIM features can differ by region.

## Why "latest" is not always "best for you"

The newest iPhone is not automatically the right buy. Consider:

- Whether you need the newest camera system
- Battery life on your current device
- Carrier promotions and trade-in value

## Where to confirm

Apple's official product page and newsroom are the authoritative sources. Retailers and carrier pages can lag or vary by region.
