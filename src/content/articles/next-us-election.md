## The short answer

US federal general elections happen **every two years**, on the **first Tuesday after the first Monday in November** in even-numbered years.

## Key dates

| Election | Year | Notes |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Midterm elections | 2026 | All 435 House seats and roughly a third of the Senate |
| Presidential election | 2028 | President and Vice President, plus House and Senate |
| Midterm elections | 2030 | House and a third of the Senate |

## What "midterm" means

A midterm election falls in the middle of a president's four-year term. It includes all House seats, many Senate seats, and numerous state and local races.

## Primary elections

Before the general election, political parties hold **primaries** or caucuses to choose candidates. These dates vary widely by state.

## State and local elections

Many state and local elections do not follow the federal calendar. Check your **state or county election office** for local dates and deadlines.

## Important

Dates can shift by state for primaries and special elections. For authoritative dates and registration deadlines, use your state election website.
