## The honest answer

There is almost never one reason Bitcoin "fell today." Crypto trades 24/7, is highly volatile, and small events can trigger outsized moves because of leverage. Any explanation should be treated as a hypothesis, not certainty.

## Common drivers of a drop

- **Macro news.** Interest-rate decisions, inflation data and dollar strength affect risk assets broadly, including Bitcoin.
- **Liquidations.** When leveraged long positions get liquidated, forced selling can accelerate a decline.
- **Regulation.** Enforcement actions or policy headlines can spook markets quickly.
- **ETF and institutional flows.** Net outflows from spot ETFs can pressure price.
- **Sentiment and correlation.** Bitcoin often trades alongside tech stocks and reacts to risk-on/risk-off shifts.

## How to check what actually happened

1. Look at a live chart to see the timing of the move.
2. Read credible market reporting for that specific day.
3. Check whether the move was broad (affecting all crypto) or Bitcoin-specific.

## Avoid confident single-cause claims

Beware of headlines that attribute a move to one tweet or one event without evidence. Markets are complex; good analysis names the likely contributors and admits uncertainty.
