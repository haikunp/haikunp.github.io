## The answer

**Mount Everest** is the tallest mountain measured **above sea level**, at **8,848.86 metres (29,031.7 ft)**. This figure comes from a 2020 joint survey by China and Nepal.

## Why people sometimes say Mauna Kea

**Mauna Kea** in Hawaii is the tallest mountain measured **from base to peak** — about 10,210 metres (33,500 ft) from the ocean floor. But roughly 6,000 metres of it is underwater, so it is not the highest above sea level.

## The distinction that matters

- **Elevation above sea level** → Everest wins.
- **Total height from base to summit** → Mauna Kea wins.

Both statements are correct depending on the definition. Most "tallest mountain" questions mean elevation above sea level, so Everest is the standard answer.

## What about K2 or Denali?

K2 is the second-highest peak above sea level (8,611 m). Denali is the highest mountain in North America (6,190 m). Neither exceeds Everest by any standard measure.
