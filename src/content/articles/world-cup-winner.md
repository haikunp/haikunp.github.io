## The answer

The **2022 FIFA World Cup** was won by **Argentina**, who beat France on penalties after a dramatic 3–3 final in Qatar. It was Argentina's third World Cup title.

## What's next

The **2026 World Cup** is co-hosted by the **United States, Canada and Mexico** — the first time three nations host together, and the first 48-team tournament.

## Why the answer changes

Because the World Cup is held every four years, "who won" always refers to the most recent completed tournament. Always check FIFA's official site for the current winner and upcoming schedule.
