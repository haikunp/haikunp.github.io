## Why this answer depends on the cycle

"Who won the midterms" is a time-sensitive question. Control of the House and Senate, and individual race winners, change with each election cycle and are not final until results are **certified** by state authorities.

## How midterm results are determined

- **House races** are won seat by seat in 435 districts.
- **Senate races** depend on which one-third of seats is up in that cycle.
- **Certification** happens after vote counting, canvassing and, where required, recounts.

## How to verify officially

For authoritative results, use:

1. Your state election office's certified results.
2. The Associated Press and major networks' race calls for early reporting (not final certification).
3. Official government sources for the final tally.

## Beware of premature claims

Unofficial projections are not the same as certified results. Check that any "winner" claim has been certified or clearly labeled as a projection.

> This page deliberately avoids stating a fixed winner because the correct answer changes with each election and must reflect certified results.
